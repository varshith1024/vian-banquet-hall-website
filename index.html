An Intimate Setting
for Life’s Finest Moments

Nestled in Champapet, Hyderabad, ViAn Banquet Hall is a thoughtfully designed space where elegance meets warmth. With a capacity of 100 guests and a refined atmosphere, every celebration here feels personal, graceful, and unforgettable.

From opulent weddings and intimate family gatherings to refined corporate events, our venue adapts beautifully — supported by air-conditioned comfort, power backup, professional lighting, and dedicated hospitality.
